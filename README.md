# Aamir Ahmad

**Computer Science Student | Data Engineering | Machine Learning**

I am a Computer Science student interested in building structured, data-driven solutions and managing the process of bringing technical ideas into practice.

My interests lie in Data Engineering and Machine Learning, particularly in transforming raw information into organized, meaningful and usable systems.

Beyond the technical implementation, I am interested in the broader process of project planning, requirements analysis, workflow design and delivery.

## Areas of Interest

### Data Engineering

I am particularly interested in the journey from raw data to structured and usable information.

- Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Transforming Data Lakes into structured Data Warehouses.
- ETL and ELT workflows.
- Database design and management.
- Data organization, modeling and representation.
- Making complex datasets understandable and accessible.

### Machine Learning

My primary interests within Machine Learning include supervised learning and Natural Language Processing.

- Supervised learning and predictive modelling.
- Model training and evaluation.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP).
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII) detection and analysis.
- Data anonymization and privacy-preserving analytics.
- Tokenization for text processing and sensitive-data protection.

### Project Management

I am interested in the organizational side of technology and software development.

- Project planning and execution.
- Requirements analysis.
- Workflow design and process organization.
- Task coordination and progress tracking.
- Technical problem-solving and project delivery.
